    * `entries_read`: total count of valid translation entries (non-empty canonical normalized key) parsed across all scanned files.
    * `unique_keys`: count of distinct canonical normalized keys (`len(by_norm_key)`).
    * `duplicate_keys`: count of distinct canonical normalized keys that have `> 1` parsed entry (`sum(1 for entries in by_norm_key.values() if len(entries) > 1)`).

  - **Required Tested Mathematical Identity:** `entries_read == unique_keys + duplicate_entries`.
