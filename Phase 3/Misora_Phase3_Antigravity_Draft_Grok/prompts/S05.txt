PART 2 — REQUIRED IMPLEMENTATION (BUG-02, BUG-05, BUG-06, HYG-04, BUG-37)
==================================================
### A. BUG-02 — never merge adjacent boxes when clearing a speaker
Capture the target primary Record.uid before mutation.
If a print<->tag kind conversion would otherwise merge adjacent boxes, insert the minimum valid empty boundary breaker first.
Rebuild the projection only AFTER the full record write. This also fixes a stale speaker after tag->print.
Clearing a speaker on a box that sits between narration boxes must leave those boxes separate.
The result must survive save/reload and Undo/Redo, with zero IndexError.
### B. BUG-05 — one ownership contract for insert, delete, and move
A voice cue (cmd_id 12 whose arg starts with vo_, immediately before the box's first segment) travels with the box on move, and is not deleted as if it were a breaker.
Remove ONLY a positively identified empty projected tap-breaker (cmd_id 10 or another verified empty breaker).
NEVER remove a voice cue or an unknown interstitial command.
Do not create double breakers. Do not merge boxes as a side effect.
BoxStructuralDelta must snapshot every affected structural record so Undo and Redo restore the exact bytes.
### C. BUG-06 — multi-segment edit, undo, and paste
Allow normal non-blocking edits of multi-segment boxes.
Show a small non-blocking indicator whose text is the segment count plus the suffix " segs" (example: 3 segs).
BoxEditDelta stores old_seg_texts and new_seg_texts. Coalesced edits update new_seg_texts too.
Undo then Redo restores the original segment splits.
on_box_paste inserts a deep-copied BoxSnapshot and keeps seg_texts, voice_cue, and extra_args when the clipboard holds an internal snapshot. Do not flatten that snapshot through DialogueBlock.
### D. HYG-04 — bound removed_records
After you confirm which undo snapshots still point at tombstones, drop or bound StoryDocument.removed_records once those records are physically gone and no active undo snapshot needs them.
If a rollback path still needs a tombstone, keep that tombstone and document why in the Walkthrough. Do not break Undo.
### E. BUG-37 — Raw Commands is read-only until S18
txt_raw must be state disabled except while _update_raw_view writes it.
Users must not be able to type into Raw Commands in this session. Typed edits would be discarded.
Do not build the S18 editor.
